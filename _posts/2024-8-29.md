## My Experience as a KDD'24 Volunteer 🌟

Participating as a volunteer at [KDD 2024](https://www.kdd.org/) was an incredible opportunity to contribute to one of the leading conferences in data mining and machine learning. From engaging with prominent researchers to assisting in organizing technical sessions, this experience has been both rewarding and inspiring.

### Activities as a Volunteer:
- Coordinated logistics for workshops and tutorials.
- Assisted speakers during their presentations.
- Helped attendees navigate through the conference venue.
