About

Building AI systems people can rely on

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, advised by Prof. Yao Ma. I previously earned my bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from Central South University.

I bring energy and technical depth to applied AI and machine learning: framing hard problems, designing careful experiments, evaluating models honestly, and communicating results clearly. I am interested in conversations about research scientist, applied scientist, and machine learning engineering opportunities.
