The big question

How can an LLM know that the evidence behind its answer is sufficient, relevant, and trustworthy?

Reliable RAG needs more than retrieving documents. My work investigates explicit retrieval planning, knowledge-graph structure, and dependable benchmarks so systems can ground their answers in evidence that can be inspected and evaluated.
